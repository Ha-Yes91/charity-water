# charity: water — Student Landing Page Campaign A high-converting, mobile-responsive landing page concept and campaign strategy designed for **charity: water** to engage college undergraduates (Gen Z). --- ## 📌 Project Overview * **Role:** Digital Marketing Associate * **Author:** Raymond Hayes * **Date:** September 2026 * **Objective:** Design a targeted digital campaign and landing page experience that lowers friction for Gen Z micro-donations through radical transparency and campus-relatable messaging. --- ## 🎯 Target Audience: Gen Z Undergraduates (Ages 18–24) | Dimension | Details | | :--- | :--- | | **Who They Are** | College students balancing coursework, entry-level jobs, student loans, and strict budgets. Digitally fluent, socially conscious, and active on TikTok and Instagram. | | **What They Seek** | Accessible, low-barrier opportunities to make an impact without pressure for large recurring pledges. | | **Key Influences** | Peer recommendations, social proof ("receipts"), creator content, and quick checkout options (Apple Pay, Venmo). | | **Friction Points** | Financial hesitation (*"Can my $5 really make a difference?"*) and institutional skepticism regarding nonprofit overhead. | | **Conversion Levers** | Relatable micro-giving framed around daily habits (e.g., the cost of an iced coffee), backed by 100% financial transparency and GPS-verified impact. | --- ## 💡 Campaign Messaging & Hook > **"Ditch one coffee. Fund a well. Get the receipts."** * **The Problem:** Over 703 million people live without safe drinking water. * **The Solution:** charity: water’s 100% model ensures private donors cover operating costs so 100% of public donations fund water projects. * **The Proof:** Every project is mapped via satellite GPS and documented with verified field photos of the exact community served. --- ## 🎨 Visual Identity & Brand System ### Color Palette | Color | Hex Code | Role & Usage | | :--- | :--- | :--- | | **Water Yellow** | `#FFC907` | Primary CTAs, focal points, brand optimism | | **Deep Navy** | `#1A365D` | Headers, foundational elements, trust/authority | | **Clean Water Blue** | `#29B6F6` | Accent elements, highlights, freshness | | **Off-White** | `#FAFAFA` | Clean background canvas for readability | | **Charcoal** | `#212529` | High-contrast body copy | ### Typography * **Headlines:** `Montserrat` (Bold / 700) — Bold, punchy, modern. * **Body Copy:** `Inter` (Regular / 400) — Clean, highly legible across mobile screens. ### Photography Principles * **Dignity Over Guilt:** Highlighting community joy, resilience, and local ownership rather than poverty tropes. * **Shared Connection:** Authentic, relatable campus peer moments. * **Proof of the Solution:** Clear imagery of operational, community-led water systems. --- ## 🚀 UX & Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) ### Key Page Features * **Tiered Micro-Donation Pills:** Preset amounts (`$5`, `$10`, `$20`, `Custom`) minimize decision fatigue, anchoring the minimum tier to the cost of a coffee. * **Primary Call-to-Action:** * `Send $5 to Clean Water` * Clarifies the precise cost, action, and outcome in a single button. * **Secondary Educational CTA:** * `See How We Track Every Dollar with GPS` * Directly resolves overhead skepticism. * **One-Tap Payments:** Direct integration with **Apple Pay** and **Venmo** eliminates checkout form fatigue. --- ## 📱 Tech Stack & Implementation (Suggested) * **Markup/Styling:** HTML5, Tailwind CSS / Sass * **Typography:** Google Fonts (`Montserrat`, `Inter`) * **Assets:** Optimized SVG icons, WebP imagery * **Payment Integrations:** Stripe Elements, Apple Pay API, Braintree / Venmo Web SDK --- ##
